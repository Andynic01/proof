The 30 days

Four phases. Three tests. One ledger.

Five training patterns rotate so no muscle group works two days running. Every seventh day is recovery. Days 1, 15 and 30 are the same four tests, so your progress is a number, not a feeling.

Inside a session

Warm up. Work. Log it. Cool down.

Warm-up, about 3 minutes

Jacks, arm circles, a deep squat hold, inchworms and cat-cow. The same every day, so it becomes automatic.

The work, 12 to 25 minutes

Circuits or straight sets. The player shows the move moving, counts down timed holds and runs your rest clock.

Log what you actually did

The target is a suggestion. You record the real number. Missing a target is data, not failure.

Rate it, get stamped

Easy, Solid or Brutal. Your rating nudges the next session up or down by 5%. Then the day gets its stamp.

The moves

The movement library

Every exercise has the cues that matter, the mistakes that hurt, and an easier and harder version. Real-person demo videos are being filmed now; until each one goes up, the move is shown by a hand-drawn guide figure. Tap one to open it.

The proof

Day 1, Day 15, Day 30

Same tests, same rules, same order. Fresh, warmed up, stop the moment form breaks. Your Proof tab puts the three results side by side.

Straight talk

What 30 days can and can't do

Realistic in 30 days

  • More push-ups, squats and plank time than on Day 1. Beginners often see the biggest jumps, mostly from better skill and coordination.
  • Cleaner technique on every move in the library
  • A habit: 26 training sessions and 4 recovery days completed
  • Knowing which level you really belong at

Not something we'll promise

  • A number on the scales. That depends far more on food, sleep and stress than on any workout plan.
  • Shocking anyone. Maybe you will. We'd rather you just beat your own Day 1.
  • Results without the reps. There is no shortcut button in this app.

Before you start: if you have a heart, joint or blood-pressure condition, are pregnant, or are coming back from injury, check with your GP or physio first. Stop any move that causes sharp pain, dizziness or chest discomfort. Burning muscles are normal; sharp joint pain is not.
